## 3. Unlock modes

- **Stand-alone build:** `NbState.gd:20` has `var full_unlock: bool = true`. The map shows all 5 levels (05).
- **Shell mode:** `set_full_unlock(false)` limits the map to levels 1-3 (13). The level 3 card has no next button and emits `free_levels_finished` once (14). `full_unlock` is not saved, and the MWM Play shell calls `enter()` before it loads the scene (`mwm-play/app/shell/Shell.gd:312` then `_change_scene`), so the map never builds with a stale flag.

## 4. Performance risk (static; no phone attached)

- **Draw calls:** about 30 in play by my count. That covers the sky, sea, 3 palms, field glass, 3 rails, 3 tubes, 2 lamps, 1 brick MultiMesh with a single 300-triangle surface, the paddle GLB, ball, halo, trail, sparks MultiMesh and net. Capsules, shards and puffs are pooled and hidden when idle.
- **Bricks:** one MultiMesh, an opaque shader (`blend_mix, depth_draw_opaque`, no ALPHA), and `use_custom_data` for hp, kind and glow. `_sync_bricks` (`NbWorld.gd:667-701`) rewrites the transform and custom data for every brick every frame. That is fine at 60-120 bricks.
- **Particles:** 8 pooled `GPUParticles3D` x 24 = 192 at most (limit 500). Komet sparks are a MultiMesh of 10.
- **Glow:** on, with levels 1-3, additive, HDR threshold 1.0. MSAA 3D is 2x. There is 1 directional light with shadows off. SSAO, SSR and fog stay at their defaults (off). The largest texture is the 512 px icon, and there are no textures inside the GLBs (10-37 KB each).
- **Transparent layers:** the field glass is a 72% black quad over almost the whole field, giving about 2-3x overdraw with sky and sea under it. The halo, trail, net, 4 puffs and shards are additive.
- **Per-frame allocations:** `NbHandHint._draw` makes 10 `StyleBoxFlat.new()` per frame while the hand shows, and for a child who never drags it shows the whole level. `NbWorld._draw_trail` makes one `Array[Vector2]` per frame (small). `NbWinCard._draw` makes 2 StyleBoxes per frame for 1 s. NbSim allocates nothing per frame except a `keep` array while capsules fall, and a string per wall or chrome hit in `_record_loop`.
- **32-bit Samsung tablet:** glow plus 2x MSAA at full resolution over a large alpha quad is the main fill-rate risk. Whether the tablet runs the Vulkan mobile path or falls back to Compatibility is unknown.
- **PerfOverlay:** the autoload is present (`project.godot`) and matches the studio template, minus the owner's name. A three-finger tap toggles it. **Phone performance: UNVERIFIED.** It needs fps, draws and VRAM from the phone and from the tablet on level 4 mid-play.

## 5. Look (premium stylized 3D tier): top 5 "prototype" tells, by visual gain per GPU ms

1. **The sun reads as muddy ochre, not neon** (01, 02: about #8a6a20 behind the 72% glass). Lift the sun disc's emission, or cut a softer glass hole behind it. This costs 0 ms because it is a shader constant.
2. **The paddle is a thin cyan outline** (01, 07). Next to the chunky glass bricks it reads as wireframe, the weakest object on screen. Give it a lit body or fill under the rim (same GLB, one material).
3. **The win card is a flat cream slab** over a dimmed neon scene (04, 14), which breaks the style. A thin neon rim or a soft glow edge on the card would fix it at almost no cost.
4. **The shell card's two discs sit off-centre** with an empty third slot (14). Centre the replay and map discs when there is no next level.
5. **There is a seam at the frame top**: a grey band at x 0-30, y 235-265 where the rail ends (01, 05), plus about 230 px of plain sky above the field on 16:9. Extend the rail past the edge, or fade the band.

Budget per DESIGN section 10: 0 shadowed lights, 1 GPU particle pool (192 max), 7 transparent or additive materials, 0 textures above 2048, and SSAO/SSR/fog off.

## 7. Findings

1. **Medium:** `scripts/NbSettings.gd:60`. The settings note "Vanskelighet gjelder fra neste bane." is 36 px, below the 40 px body floor. Set `font_size` to 40 or more.
2. **Medium (perf):** `scripts/NbHandHint.gd:54-59`. `_round_rect` allocates a new `StyleBoxFlat` 10 times per frame while the hint shows, which is the whole level for an idle child. Build the 10 styleboxes once in `_ready`, or draw with `draw_circle`/`draw_rect`.
3. **Low:** `docs/mockups/src/label_sheet.py:7`. A local absolute path (`/home/<user>/...`) sits in a public repo. Use a path relative to the repo, or an environment variable.
4. **Low (spec drift):** `scripts/NbSettings.gd:44-57` against GDD 10.2 (`docs/GDD.md:425`). The panel has sound and "Mindre bevegelse" but no music or vibration rows. `music_on` and `haptics_on` are stored but unused. Either add the rows when music and haptics exist, or update the GDD.
5. **Low:** `scripts/NbWinCard.gd:31-33,54`. With no next level (shell level 3), the replay and map discs stay at x 270/540 and leave a gap on the right (14). Re-centre them at x 360/720 when `has_next` is false.
6. **Low (child safety of debug UI):** `scripts/PerfOverlay.gd:24-33`. A child's palm or three fingers toggles yellow debug text at (24, 96), on top of the home disc. Accept it as owner-only, or require a hold of about 1 s.
7. **Low (test tooling):** `tests/capture.gd:49-52`. If a save file already exists, the first launch opens the map, and the bot then crashes with `SCRIPT ERROR: Invalid access to property 'paddle_x' on Nil` instead of reporting the cause. I hit this on a reused user dir. Assert `main.screen == "play"` and print "stale save in user dir", or delete the save at bot start.
8. **Low (test tooling):** `tests/net_test.gd` exits with "ObjectDB instances leaked" and "3 resources still in use". This is likely the static `NbMeshes._brick_body` plus the sims. Clear the static mesh before `quit()` so real leaks are not hidden.
9. **Info:** `scripts/NbLevelDisc.gd:21-27`. All 5 map discs run `_process` and `queue_redraw` every frame, even while the map is hidden in play. Only the suggested disc needs it.
10. **Info (design):** in Lett, an idle paddle clears level 1 faster (48-83 s) than the tracking bot (95 s). The rules are met, but skill is not visibly rewarded at Lett. This is the game designer's call.
11. **Info (integration):** the game sets `msaa_3d=2` in `project.godot`. The MWM Play adapter defaults to `MSAA_DISABLED` (`ShellAdapter.gd`), so set `msaa_3d` in the neon-bricks adapter on purpose.
12. **Info (look):** see section 5, items 1-5.
