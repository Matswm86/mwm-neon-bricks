## 5. Performance (static, plus a draw-call breakdown measured on the laptop)

**Draw calls, where they go.** I paused the tree and hid each node in turn (Xvfb, NVIDIA; my measurement). With live particles the capture shows 51-78:

| Part | Draws | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **NbComboMeter (top progress bar)** | **19** | The biggest single cost. `_draw()` (`NbComboMeter.gd:96-125`) alternates `draw_rect` fill, lit fill, outline and rush outline over 10 segments, and each switch between filled rects and line rects starts a new canvas batch. |
| Home disc + gear (NbHomeDisc x2) | 6 + 6 | circle, anti-aliased arc, ring fill arc, icon polygons |
| 3D scene | 24-34 | about 1 per node: sky, sea, field glass, 8 rails and tubes, the brick MultiMesh, paddle, ball, halo, trail, net, 2-5 vista MultiMeshes, boss, portals |
| 8 pooled GPUParticles3D | 1 each, **idle or not** | `emitting=false` emitters still draw while `visible` |
| Glow | 0 | not counted by this monitor |

So 2D UI costs a constant 32 draws, more than half the budget, against the "UI 5" line in DESIGN 7e.
**What to cut, in order:**
1. **Batch the combo meter** (saves about 16). Draw all 10 OFF fills, then all lit fills, then every edge in one `draw_multiline` (or one `draw_polyline` per row), then the rush glow the same way. Grouping by primitive type keeps it at 3-4 batches. Or draw the bar as one textured quad with a small shader.
2. **Hide idle particle emitters** (saves up to 8). Set `visible = false` when a pooled `GPUParticles3D` finishes and back to `true` on `restart()`.
3. **Home and gear as cached textures** (saves about 10). Render each disc once into an `ImageTexture` or `ViewportTexture` and `draw_texture` it, or merge them into one Control that draws both.

Doing 1 and 2 alone puts the worst capture (78) at about 54, inside 60.

**Other static checks**
- Renderer: `mobile`, not Forward+. There is 1 DirectionalLight3D with `shadow_enabled = false` (`NbWorld.gd:199`) and no omni or spot lights. SSAO, SSR, fog and SDFGI are not set (defaults off).
- Textures: none above 512 px (PIL scan of `assets/` plus `icon.png`), so none come near 2048.
- Particles: 8 x 40 at Neonrush (`SHARDS_RUSH`) = 320 at most, under 500. DESIGN 7e says 24 x 6 alive (144). The Rush tier is over the design ceiling, not the QA limit.
- Per-frame allocations: none in any `_process`, `_sync*` or `_draw` of play code. The slice's `NbHandHint` and `NbWinCard` StyleBox allocations are gone. One remains in `NbSettings.gd:207` (`_draw`, `StyleBoxFlat.new()`), but it runs only when the settings panel redraws.
- Transparent or additive materials: field glass, ghost MultiMesh, ball halo, trail, echo balls, puffs, net, plus world 4 lamp pools and world 5 mist cards. All are allowed by DESIGN 7e and 11.6.
- **PerfOverlay:** the autoload is present (`project.godot`) and opens on a 1 s three-finger hold. **Phone performance: UNVERIFIED.** Mats needs to read fps, draws and VRAM on L26 (worst, 78 draws) and L30 mid-fight, on the phone and on the 32-bit tablet.

DESIGN section 10 budget: 0 shadowed lights, 1 particle pool (8 emitters, 320 max), about 10 transparent or additive materials, 0 textures above 2048, SSAO/SSR/fog off.

## 6. Look (premium stylized 3D): five biggest prototype tells, by visual gain per GPU ms

1. **Capsules read as blank teal pills** for half of every spin (31, 35, 37, 39, 22, 23: no icon visible). A non-reader cannot tell what will drop. Put the icon on both faces of `capsule_*.glb`, or spin only around the long axis so the icon face stays toward the camera. Cost 0.
2. **The world 2 moon and the world 6 planet sit half behind the gear** (31, 23, 25, 28; 38, 39, 42, 43). The gear stays readable thanks to its dark ring, but the world's signature is cut in half. Move the motif left: moon `motif_c.x` from 0.2 to about 0.07 (screen centre near x 635), planet x from 104 to about 60. Both should end left of x 860. This changes DESIGN 11.1, so graphic-designer owns it. Cost 0.
3. **Map pages keep the last level's tube colour.** They show yellow after world 3 (27-29) and Neonrush white-hot after L30 (40-43), never the page's own world colour. `_sync_tubes` runs only in play, and `set_world()` sets `_tube_base` without applying it. In `set_world()`, reset `_combo` and `_rush` and write `_tube_mat[i].albedo_color = _tube_base * _tube_gain`. Cost 0.
4. **Magnet marks vanish on the sun-yellow row** (38, `crop_magnets.png`: white chevrons on pale yellow, about 1.1:1). The ghost marks solved the same problem with a dark keyline (DESIGN 12.2). Add a `#1A0614` keyline under the magnet ring and chevrons in `brick.gdshader` kind 8. Cost 0. This is also a child-rule item (finding 4).
5. **The world 1-3 bosses are still the orange placeholder slab** (22, 23, 24) next to the drawn truck, gem and Neonnova (35, 37, 39). That breaks the "finished" read in the first half of the game. They need graphic-designer GLBs for L5, L10 and L15. Cost: a model swap, same draw count.

## 8. Findings

1. **Medium (perf):** `scripts/NbComboMeter.gd:96-125`. 19 draw calls for 10 segments. Batch by primitive type (all fills, then all edges in one `draw_multiline`). This is the biggest cut toward the 60 budget.
2. **Medium (perf):** `scripts/NbWorld.gd` particle pool (about line 533). Idle `GPUParticles3D` cost 1 draw each. Set `visible=false` when an emitter finishes and `true` on restart.
3. **Medium (design/endless):** `scripts/NbEndless.gd:136-137` (`width <= 8`) against the mirrored templates (`_template_has`). March blocks never appear in endless (0/180). Decide the rule in GDD 16.9 rule 6, then fix the generator. Add a test for march frequency over k 1-200 (expect about 30-40% of non-boss levels).
4. **Medium (child rule 36):** `shaders/brick.gdshader` kind 8 (magnet). Its marks are about 1.1:1 on the sun-yellow row (38). Add a dark keyline as on the ghost marks (DESIGN 12.2).
5. **Low (look):** DESIGN 11.1 and 11.6 put the moon (x 710-910) and planet (x 800-960) under the gear. Shift them left (section 6, item 2). The owner is graphic-designer.
6. **Low (look):** `scripts/NbWorld.gd:624-643` (`set_world`). Map pages keep the last level's tube colour and Neonrush white. Reset `_combo` and `_rush` and apply `_tube_base` at once.
7. **Low (look):** capsules show no icon for half of every spin (section 6, item 1).
8. **Low (perf):** home and gear discs cost 6 draws each. Cache them as textures.
9. **Low (flash rule, reading):** `scripts/NbPieces.gd:203-206` and `NbWorld.gd:1286-1292`. One limiter grant shows two soft pulses (2 Hz, glow +0.6) during the ghost warning, so a warning plus 2 other grants in the same second can show 4 brightenings. That matches GDD 16.2.1 as written, and 2 Hz is below the 3 Hz line. If you want it airtight, count each pulse as a grant, or drop to one pulse.
10. **Low (data):** `tests/levels_test.gd` `SIM_MEDIAN` Lett for L16, 21, 23, 25 and 30 is 10-56% below the game. Refresh it from this run, or note in GDD 16.5 that the Lett sim is optimistic.
11. **Low (test coverage):** endless march frequency, the map tube colour and the endless page under `full_unlock(false)` are not in any test. I proved the last one with a probe (section 3). Add it to `net_test`.
12. **Info:** exit leak lines remain for `levels_test` (10 resources) and a plain `--quit` (2). The headless `material_get_instance_shader_parameters: material is null` (x3) appears only with the dummy renderer in the flash group, never under Xvfb.
13. **Info:** the endless page shows "continue" and "start at 1" even when `endless_best` is 0, and both start k 1. Hide "continue" until `endless_best >= 2`.
14. **Info:** the APK is 184 MB (193 218 624 bytes), up from 153 MB. Check what grew before a store build (the GLBs added in 12.8 are small, so look at the music and the nebula texture).
